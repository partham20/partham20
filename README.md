### by day

I write firmware that has to survive a power cut mid-update — bootloaders, CAN-FD
stacks, OTA pipelines on TI C2000 and embedded Linux. The kind of code where
"it mostly works" is the same as "it doesn't."

### by whatever's left

Three books, a poetry collection, letters I probably shouldn't have published,
and a podcast called **[My Inner Monologue](https://open.spotify.com/show/0lUJhwd4aBgPJ5DWFyTCu4)** —
one long attempt to answer *why do I do what I do?* out loud.

All of it lives at **[parthasarathym.com](https://parthasarathym.com)** — a digital
replica of my entire life. Password-protected; knock first.
